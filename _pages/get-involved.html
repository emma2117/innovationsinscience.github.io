If you're inspired by the Innovations in Science mission and want to get involved, there are ways you can help! If you:

  • are a scientist eager to share your work with local publication,
  • are a journalist eager to report on local science,
  • are aware of resources, financial or otherwise, that could help us expand our model, or
  • just generally want to help...

We would love to hear from you! Please reach out to us at community@innovationsinscience.com or fill out the form below.
